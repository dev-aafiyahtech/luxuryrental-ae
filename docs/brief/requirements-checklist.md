# Requirements Checklist

What the client needs to supply to turn the prototype into the production site (issued 23 Oct 2026).
The client's answers are in [requirements-answers.md](requirements-answers.md).

## Send first (these start the work)
- [ ] Logo and brand files in vector format (SVG or AI), plus any brand colours or fonts already in use
- [ ] Fleet list: make, model, year, colour, category and number of each car
- [ ] The 3–5 hero cars to build in 3D for the Kinetic Sculpture and Burj by Night scenes
- [ ] Prices per car: daily, weekly and monthly rates, deposit, included km per day, charge per extra km
- [ ] How bookings are taken today (spreadsheet, WhatsApp, rental software, or nothing yet)

## Visuals
- [ ] 3D models: budget to buy licensed models of the hero cars, or to hire a 3D artist
- [ ] Photos and video: a shoot of the real cars (studio and Dubai at night), or approval to book one
- [ ] Real customer reviews, if a testimonials section is wanted

## Booking rules
- [ ] Minimum rental period and how far in advance people must book
- [ ] Delivery zones and fees, airport handover, delivery hours
- [ ] Driver rules: minimum age, accepted licences, which countries need an international permit, documents to upload
- [ ] Insurance options; fuel, Salik, traffic-fine and damage policies
- [ ] Cancellation and refund policy
- [ ] Chauffeur service pricing and how bookings for it work

## Payments and legal
- [ ] Trade licence and RTA car-rental permit (payment providers ask for these)
- [ ] Payment provider: existing account, or approval to open one with Stripe, Checkout.com or Network International
- [ ] VAT registration number, and whether prices are shown with VAT included
- [ ] Rental agreement, terms and conditions, and privacy policy (to be reviewed by a UAE lawyer)

## Content
- [ ] Business name, address, phone, WhatsApp number and opening hours
- [ ] "About us" story, FAQs and social media links
- [ ] Languages: English and Arabic planned; others? Who translates or checks the Arabic?

## Access and accounts
- [ ] Access to the registrar account for luxuryrentals.ae
- [ ] Existing site address, if any, so search rankings carry over
- [ ] Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Google Business Profile and WhatsApp Business accounts
- [ ] Who should get logins to update the fleet and prices

## Decisions
- [ ] Target launch date and overall budget
- [ ] One person who approves designs and content
- [ ] One contact on the operations side for booking-rule questions
